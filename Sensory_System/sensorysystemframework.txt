SYSTEM FLOW SUMMARY
===================

1. MAIN.PY launches the hub, sets up CameraManager and CONTEXT.
2. CameraManager continuously feeds frames to IdleDetection and ObjFaceRecog.
3. IdleDetectionCombined publishes user presence, gaze, and activity state.
4. AppUsageMonitor tracks active window focus and duration, publishing to CONTEXT.
5. ObjFaceRecog identifies people and posts recognized names to CONTEXT.
6. ContextFusion acts as the message bus — everything communicates through it.
7. ProactiveOutputs (NaturalResponder) subscribes to context events, decides when to speak, log, or act.
8. Output goes to text-to-speech, console, or ARGUS GUI/HUD.


THREAD / DATA FLOW OVERVIEW
===========================

[Camera Thread]
  └─> CameraManager.read() → frame buffer
         ├─> IdleDetectionCombined (analyzes gaze/presence)
         ├─> ObjFaceRecog (identifies faces)
         └─> ContextFusion.update(...)

[System Thread]
  └─> AppUsageMonitor.poll() → active_app, duration → ContextFusion

[Responder Thread]
  └─> ContextFusion.on_change() → NaturalResponder callback
         ├─> evaluate activity_state / faces / app / weather
         ├─> speak_fn("take a break", "hello <name>", etc)
         └─> log context change to CSV

[Console Thread]
  └─> ContextPrinter(snapshot) → prints summary
